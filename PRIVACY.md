# Mixquilt Privacy Policy

_Last updated: November 11, 2026_

Mixquilt is provided by Stephen Curran III, New York.

Mixquilt is a song studio that runs entirely in your web browser (or as an installed app). This policy explains, in plain English, what happens to your information.

## The short version

- **Your songs stay on your device.** Everything you make is saved in your browser's own storage on this device. It is never uploaded to us or to anyone else.
- **No accounts.** You don't sign up or log in, and we never ask for your name, email or phone number.
- **No analytics, tracking, ads or cookies.** Mixquilt has no analytics or advertising code, no tracking pixels and no third-party scripts. Mixquilt itself sets no cookies.
- **Microphone only when you record.** The app asks for microphone access only when you tap Record or the latency Auto-measure button. It stops listening as soon as you stop.

## What is stored, and where

Mixquilt keeps the following in your browser's storage (IndexedDB and local storage) on your device only:

- your songs: recipe choices, beats, melodies, lyrics, arrangement, mix settings and the authorship log;
- your recorded vocal takes, and any Voice Polish versions of them;
- a few app preferences, such as your latency adjustment and which ideas "Surprise me" recently suggested.

You can delete a take or a song in the app at any time. Clearing your browser's site data for Mixquilt, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of it. We can't recover it for you, because we never had a copy.

Files you export (WAV, stems, MIDI, PDF) are saved wherever you choose. What happens to them after that is up to you.

## Microphone

Recording uses your device's microphone through the browser's standard permission prompt. Audio is captured only while you are recording or measuring latency. It is processed on your device (including Voice Polish, which is classic signal processing, not AI) and saved only on your device.

## Hosting

Mixquilt itself collects nothing: the app has no server of its own and sends nothing about you anywhere. The app's files are hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may log technical data, such as your IP address, when your browser downloads the app. GitHub handles that data under its own [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). After the first visit, Mixquilt can work offline from your device's cache.

## Children

Mixquilt is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under 13. It does not collect personal information from anyone, including children. Because nothing leaves your device, no personal information about a child is collected or stored by us.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted in the app and in the project repository with a new date.
